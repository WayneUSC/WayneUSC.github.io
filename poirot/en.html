Case Briefing

April 12, 2032. Demo night at the Qiming Robotics Lab. Director Fang Yuan straps into the lab's upper-limb force-feedback exoskeleton to demonstrate their newest haptic teleoperation system. At 21:58, the elbow joint outputs a catastrophic torque spike — and the safety limiter never engages. Fang Yuan does not survive.

The police call it an equipment failure. But the lab's social robot, POIROT-7, refuses to sign off on the incident report. Its memory was deliberately corrupted around the time of the incident, it says — yet the surviving fragments tell it one thing: this was no accident.

You are the independent investigator. POIROT-7 will serve as your game master — guiding your search, arranging interrogations, and doing something no human host would dare: openly displaying its real-time model of you. Every estimate it maintains about your mind is rendered live in the panel on the right.

▸ Note: POIROT watches where you linger and whom you suspect. When it judges that you are stuck, it will offer help — exactly as a good human game master would.
